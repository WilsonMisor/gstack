# Current External Research Policy

Project-init marks `RESEARCH_REQUIRED` for volatile facts that cannot safely come from static skill knowledge: law/regulation/licensing/tax/custody and jurisdiction-specific duties; vendor pricing/quotas/API/deprecation/availability/terms; current CVEs/security advisories/supply-chain events; app-store/platform policies; regional/data-residency constraints. Research must distinguish source-derived facts, inference and qualified-human review requirements. Lack of current research remains UNKNOWN and cannot silently become GREEN.

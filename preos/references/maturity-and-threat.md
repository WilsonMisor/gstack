# Maturity and Threat Models

Product maturity: 0 Prototype (validation/low cost); 1 Early Production (real fundamentals); 2 Product Traction (measured-growth controls); 3 Scaling Product (evidence-backed scaling architecture); 4 Large Platform (advanced distributed systems where justified); 5 High Assurance/Global Platform (advanced resilience/compliance/security/operations where required).

Threat growth: 0 low visibility; 1 generic scanners/spam/credential attacks/scraping; 2 economically motivated abuse/referral/OTP/account/payment fraud; 3 targeted ATO/DDoS/API exploitation/insider/organized fraud; 4 strategic attacks/supply-chain compromise/extortion/highly targeted social engineering/advanced fraud. Security investment evolves with measured value and threat.

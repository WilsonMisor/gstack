# Economics, Economic Abuse, and Deferred Complexity

Measure fixed/variable and per-user/tenant/request/transaction/success/verification/notification/GB/support-case/AI-operation/report/job/search costs, plus logs/traces/backup/recovery/engineering complexity/specialist personnel/toil/vendor lock-in/migration/failure. Identify cost concentration and abnormal cost as incidents.

Economic Abuse Surface: ask whether an attacker can cause materially more defender spend than attacker spend (SMS/OTP, AI inference, identity verification, storage/egress/search/maps/payment fees/support). Use per-actor ceilings, rate limits, budgets, circuit breakers or manual review where justified.

Deferred Complexity record: technology/control, why not needed now, current simpler solution, risk of deferral, measurable activation trigger, migration path, expected cost at activation, review condition and owners. Never deploy hyperscale technology merely as best-practice theater.
